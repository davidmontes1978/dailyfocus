Transparency

Privacy Policy

We want you to understand what information Daily Focus uses, why it is used and how you can control it.

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Data we process

Daily Focus processes the information you enter to manage tasks, routines, tracking, reminders, lists, preferences and your profile. Activity history may contain sensitive information if you write it in titles, notes or tracking entries.

Purpose and storage

We use this data to provide the app's features, synchronize it across your devices and show the statistics you request. Data is stored locally and, when you sign in, in Supabase with user access controls. Optional Google Calendar integration is only enabled when you authorize it.

Health Connect, steps and distance

Health Connect integration is optional. If enabled, Daily Focus requests only permission to read steps and distance. It uses aggregated daily totals to show progress and complete linked routines. Daily Focus does not write to Health Connect or request heart rate, sleep, weight, location or other health categories.

Retention and deletion

You can delete synchronized data or permanently delete your account from Profile in the app. You can also use our account deletion page.

Your choices

You can access, correct, export and delete your data from the app. For questions, email davidmontes1978@gmail.com.
