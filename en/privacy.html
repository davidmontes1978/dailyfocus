Transparency

Privacy Policy

We want you to understand what information Daily Focus uses, why it is used and how you can control it.

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Data we process

Daily Focus processes the data required to create and manage your account and provide its productivity features. This may include your name or display name, email address, user identifier, profile photo and the content you create in the app, such as tasks, routines, generic tracking entries, reminders, lists, titles, notes, preferences and progress.

Purpose and storage

We use this data to provide the app's features, maintain your session, synchronize your content across devices, restore it when you sign in again and display the statistics and progress you request. Data is stored locally and, when you use an account, in Supabase with access controls associated with each user.

Google Calendar

Google Calendar integration is optional and is enabled only when you authorize it. Daily Focus uses the permissions you grant solely to provide the calendar features requested by the user. You can revoke those permissions through your Google account or the relevant device settings.

User-entered content

Daily Focus text fields are free-form. Avoid entering information you do not want to store or synchronize. Daily Focus is not designed to provide medical services, diagnose conditions or manage health data.

Public data

Your display name, selected avatar, profile photo and calculated score may appear in social or ranking features when applicable. Your tasks, routines, tracking entries, reminders, lists, notes and email address are not made public.

Photos

Your profile photo is optional. If you choose to use one, it may be stored in services associated with your account so it can appear in your profile and relevant app features.

Retention and deletion

You can delete content from the app and permanently delete your account from Profile. You can also request deletion through our account deletion page. Account deletion removes the associated data we maintain to provide the service, including the profile photo when applicable, except for data we may need to retain temporarily for legal or security obligations.

Exports

Export features may include user-created content such as titles, descriptions or card definitions. They do not include passwords or authentication credentials.

Security

Communications with Daily Focus remote services use encrypted connections in transit. We apply user-based access controls to limit access to synchronized data.

Your choices

You can access, correct, export and delete your data from the app. For questions, email davidmontes1978@gmail.com.
