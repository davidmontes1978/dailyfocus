Account control

Request account deletion

You can delete your account and associated data directly in Daily Focus, or ask us to process the request by email.

Important: deletion is permanent. Your account, synchronized data and profile photo will be deleted.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Daily Focus and sign in.
  2. Open Profile and then Privacy and data.
  3. Select Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: by email

Send an email from the address linked to your account. Include your Daily Focus username and clearly state that you want to delete your account.

Request deletion by email ↗

Do not send passwords or access codes.
